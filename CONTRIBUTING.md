# Contributing

Pixel Gaming Studio is source-available under its own [license](LICENSE),
not open source. Read this before sending anything.

**What a contribution means.** By submitting a change for inclusion (a pull
request, or a link to your fork or patch sent to the author), you grant the
Licensor a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free, sublicensable
license to use, modify, distribute and **relicense** that change as part of
the Software, under this or any other license, including commercial ones,
plus a patent license for it (section 4 of the LICENSE). You keep your
copyright; you do not keep control over how the project licenses your change
later. If that is not acceptable to you, do not submit.

**Forks.** Public forks are fine to propose changes back, labelled as
unofficial. They are not a way to distribute the engine or to offer it as a
service (sections 2.4 and 3 of the LICENSE).

**What is welcome.** A capability the engine lacks, with a test that shows
the need; a fix with the failing case; a documentation correction. Before a
large change, open an issue: the engine grows from concrete needs of games
being made with it, and the roadmap files say what is next.

**How.** Read `AGENTS.md` first. Keep `npm run lint` at zero errors and
`npm test` green. One change per pull request, with a line in `CHANGELOG.md`
that says what it is and where it came from.
